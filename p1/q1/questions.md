# AI Problem Discovery - Requirements Interview

Opening

Thank you for taking the time today. Before discussing AI solutions, I'd like to understand your current lending process and what "better lending decisions" means from your perspective.

### Questions

1. Can you walk me through your current lending workflow, from the moment a customer submits an application until the final approval or rejection? Which teams and systems are involved?

2. When you say you want "better lending decisions", what business outcome matters the most—reducing defaults, approving more deserving customers, improving turnaround time, ensuring consistency across branches, meeting regulatory requirements, or something else?

3. Looking at the last six months, can you describe one lending decision that you believe should have gone differently? What happened, and why do you think it happened?

4. Which metrics do you currently use to measure lending performance? For example, default rate, approval rate, turnaround time, customer complaints, audit observations, or branch-level consistency.

5. Are there particular customer segments, loan products, or regions where the current decision-making process performs noticeably worse than others?

6. What level of explanation does a loan decision require for customers, auditors, and RBI compliance? Are there situations where every approval or rejection must be fully justified?

### Conditional Follow-up 1

**Trigger:** If the VP says the biggest issue is reducing loan defaults.

**Question:** Which types of borrowers contribute most to defaults? Are these first-time borrowers, specific industries, particular loan sizes, or certain geographic regions?

**Why:** Understanding where defaults originate helps determine whether the problem is caused by missing data, changing customer behaviour, or weaknesses in the current credit policy.

### Conditional Follow-up 2

**Trigger:** If the VP says approvals are taking too long.

**Question:** Where exactly does the delay occur—document verification, manual credit review, fraud checks, policy approvals, or customer communication? Which step consumes the most time?

**Why:** This distinguishes whether the bottleneck is operational or analytical. Automating a slow workflow is very different from improving a credit-risk model.
